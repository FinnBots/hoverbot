HOW TO PLAY

  • GOAL: fly down the shaft and reach the trapped bot at the bottom. Twenty shafts, then they go round again, harder.
  • ▲ / W / Up runs the rotor pack, ◀ ▶ fly. The pack burns your lamp — that bar is your clock. Empty lamp = a life.
  • BEAM (Space) fires sideways from the helmet: it kills critters and burns through stone. It cannot fire downward.
  • CHG (Down / B) drops a blast charge — only while standing. 1.5 second fuse, and it will kill you too. Six per shaft.
  • Glowing ember walls and slag are instant death. Lamps light the dark shafts — shoot one and you fly blind.
  • SCORE: critter 50 · stone tile 25 · rescue 1000, plus 10 per lamp unit left and 50 per unused charge. Extra life every 20,000.
